Wallo

Wallo is my personal space for the things I collect from the internet. Helps me track prices of the items I'm lurking, bookmark articles, and build out my visual board.

Android app Hover to play

Wishlist and bookmarks

The dock switches between the two lists. Wishlist holds the clothes. Bookmarks holds the articles.

Saving a product

Wallo pulls the product name and price from the url and saves it to the wishlist.

Price tracking

Wallo automatically checks the price every 12 hours, keeps a price history, and notifies me when it drops.

Find similar

Quick way to find the product from other retailers

Bookmark folders

Organize articles and link into folders for quick access
