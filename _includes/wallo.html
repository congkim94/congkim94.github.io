Wallo

Wallo is my personal space for the things I collect from the internet. Helps me track prices of the items I'm lurking, bookmark articles, and build out my visual board.

Android app Hover to play

Spaces

The dock switches between three spaces. Wishlist holds the clothes. Bookmarks holds the articles. Images holds the visual board.

Price tracking

Saved products are automatically price checked every 12 hours.

Find similar

Quick way to find the product from other retailers.

Content summary

Saved links and videos get a short summary.
